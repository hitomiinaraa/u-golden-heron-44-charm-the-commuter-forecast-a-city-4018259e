Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e): one line, at most 140 characters, of weather forecast for a city that does not exist — made to charm a bored commuter.

> Skytown forecast: a gentle rain of confetti, tailwinds on every platform, and your train arriving exactly when you do.

(118 characters.)

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Prints the entry to stdout and writes it byte-identically to `/output/entry.txt`.
